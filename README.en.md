## Core capabilities

- **AMD & NVIDIA—harness GPUs for audio computing** — Dual AMD HIP and NVIDIA CUDA backends offload compute for supported upsampling and convolution tasks. The UI clearly distinguishes GPU availability from whether the current track is actively hardware-accelerated.
- **Dynamics, quality, and transcoding clues—all on one page** — Centrally inspect loudness, dynamic range, true peak, clipping, and spectral analysis. Evaluates cutoff frequencies, spectral roll-off steepness, and confidence levels to highlight clues of lossy transcoding or bandwidth limits.
- **20-band EQ—tailor the sound to your preference** — Independently adjust frequency, gain, Q factor, and filter type per band with real-time responsive curve dragging. Integrated with OPRA headphone calibration presets, auto-preamp, and bypass to balance correction with personal taste.
- **Tailor your speakers to your room acoustics** — Compensate for frequency response anomalies caused by room acoustics and speaker placement via a guided measurement wizard and correction filters. Supports microphone calibration files, REW / AutoEq parametric EQ text, and WAV impulse responses.
- **Discover deeper context behind every track** — Integrated AI assists in searching and curating album, track, artist, and composition metadata. Structured presentation and local caching make musical backgrounds effortless to discover and revisit.
- **Bring album art and stories to your library** — Combines embedded audio tags, local images, and online data sources to complete covers, lyrics, and track information. Supports synchronized scrolling lyrics and metadata curation to keep your collection complete and organized.
- **Stream directly to your streamers and speakers** — Cast music to UPnP / DLNA compatible devices on your local network to integrate your PC library into existing audio systems; macOS version additionally supports AirPlay casting.
- **Connect EMBY, Navidrome and NAS** — Mount NAS drives and SMB/NFS shares directly, connect Emby, Navidrome and Plex music servers, and browse or play cloud libraries alongside local files without downloading them first.
